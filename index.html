Architectural Practice & Design Philosophy in Erode

Named after the classical Tamil title for master architects and builders (“Nedunthatchan”), our practice approaches every plot in Erode as a singular response to sun path, prevailing winds, family rituals, and Vastu-compliant spatial balance. Rather than importing generic glass facades unsuited for Tamil Nadu’s warm climate, we sculpt deep cantilevered overhangs, open-to-sky internal courtyards (muttrams), breathable terracotta and stone screens, and double-height stack-ventilated living spaces.

  • Licensed Architectural Leadership: Directed by Ar. Karthikraja G (B.Arch, Council of Architecture Registration: CA/2022/149864).
  • Single-Point Accountability: Integrated architectural planning, structural coordination, bespoke interiors, and turnkey construction under one studio.
  • Vastu & Climatic Harmony: Scientific solar orientation and cross-ventilation seamlessly aligned with traditional Vastu principles.
  • Cost & Material Transparency: Detailed Bills of Quantities (BOQ), certified material specifications, and stage-wise on-site inspections.

Our Four-Stage Design-to-Handover Process

  1. Site Feasibility, Microclimate & Vastu Analysis: Measuring plot topography, road access, wind direction, solar exposure, and local setback rules.
  2. Concept Planning & 3D Architectural Visualisation: Developing schematic floor plans, spatial zoning, and photorealistic exterior/interior 3D views.
  3. Working Drawings & Building Plan Approval: Issuing coordinated structural, electrical, plumbing, and joinery blueprints alongside municipal approval sets.
  4. On-Site Execution, PMC & Turnkey Handover: Conducting regular principal architect inspections, quality audits, and final key handover.
